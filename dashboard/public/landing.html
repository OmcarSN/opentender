HOW IT WORKS
01 SEALED BIDS
Prototype on MST Testnet. Test tokens only.
EVERYTHING A TENDER NEEDS.
ENFORCED BY CODE.
ON ONE CONTRACT.

From sealed bids to the final payment, every rule is enforced by one smart contract on MST Blockchain.

It cannot stop companies agreeing prices privately, and it relies on an honest registrar and verifiers. It is a prototype on a test network.

SEALED
BIDS
Companies lock their price in a sealed bid with a deposit. It cannot be read early or edited after submission.
ANTI-COLLUSION
GROUPS
Companies registered in the same group cannot bid against each other on the same tender.
AUTOMATIC
WINNER
After the opening, the lowest valid bid wins by rule. Ties go to the earliest sealed bid. No one picks the winner.
STAGED
PAYMENTS
The department locks the winning amount first. Each stage is paid when enough verifiers approve it. Late work is penalised automatically.
PUBLIC
AUDIT TRAIL
Every bid, award and payment is recorded on MST Testnet and can be checked by anyone in the explorer.
